Research themes

My research explores the fate of organic contaminants across agrosystems, from organic wastes (sources) to soils, water, air, plants and biota.

Organic contaminants in agroecosystems

Investigation of the occurrence, behaviour, persistence, transfer and the ecotoxicology of trace organic contaminants in agrosystems.

PharmaceuticsPFASPBDEPAHs / PCBs

Other organic contaminants
Organic waste recycling

Assessment of contaminant inputs and transformations associated with composts, digestates and other recycled organic products applied to agricultural soils.

Biogeochemical processes

Interactions between contaminants, soil organic matter, minerals, dissolved organic matter and environmental conditions.

Tropical and overseas agroecosystems

Understanding how climate, seasonality and agricultural practices influence contaminant dynamics in tropical environments.

Environmental risk and exposure

From occurrence to risk: linking chemical measurements with exposure pathways and ecotoxicological indicators.
