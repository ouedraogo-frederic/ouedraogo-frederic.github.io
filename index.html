About me

Research at the interface of chemistry, soil and agroecosystems.

My work focuses on understanding the behaviour, mobility, persistence and environmental fate of trace organic contaminants. I combine environmental chemistry, biogeochemistry and ecotoxicological approaches to investigate contaminant transfer across agricultural compartments.

Research themes

What I work on

Organic contaminants

Pesticides, PFAS, PPCPs and other trace organic contaminants in soils, water and agricultural systems.

Waste-to-soil pathways

Fate and environmental risks associated with composts, digestates and other organic amendments.

Tropical agroecosystems

Contaminant dynamics under tropical environmental conditions, including research in overseas territories.

Environmental risk

Connecting contaminant occurrence, mobility, exposure and ecological consequences across source-to-receptor pathways.
